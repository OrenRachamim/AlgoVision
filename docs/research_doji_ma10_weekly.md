# A doji sitting on the 10-week moving average (weekly bars)

Event: a weekly candle whose body is at most 10% of its high-low range (range at least 0.25% of the close) with the 10-week simple moving average of closes inside the candle's low-high range; at most one event per stock per 2 weeks. Returns are close-to-close from the doji's close at 1, 4 and 12 weeks (and from the next open in a separate table). *xloc* = the return minus the mean return of 10 random weeks of the same stock within +-26 weeks over the same horizon, *xspy* = minus the SPY over the same window, *hit* = share of positive returns, *med* = median, *t* = t-statistic of xloc. 6248 events in 511 stocks, 2017-11-10 to 2026-07-17; the main window is the last 52 weeks (2025-10-17 to 2026-10-16, 602 events); the ten-year train < 2023-01-01 / test >= 2023-01-01 split is the robustness check. Weekly bars are Monday-to-Friday aggregates of the daily cache (open = first, high = max, low = min, close = last); weeks with fewer than 3 sessions and the current unfinished week are dropped. The context splits use the same bar counts (MA10 = 10 weeks, MA50 = 50 weeks, ATR20 = 20 weeks, the 5-bar move before = 5 weeks).
