*MACD pre-touch*: will the daily MACD line (12/26) reach its signal line (9) at the next close, while the lines are still apart today (gap above 5% of |signal|)? The cell shows the probability, the close above which the lines touch tomorrow and the move it needs from the last close (negative = they touch even on a drop that size), and today's gap; bold when the probability is 70% or more; "touching" / "above signal" when there is nothing to forecast. Calibration from the AdaptiveMACD project (fitted on 2.2M US stock-days; 2015-2026 out of sample, at >= 70% about 82% of the flagged stocks touched or crossed the next day). It predicts the movement of the indicator lines, not the price direction, and has no tested link to returns here: context, not a filter or a rank. Computed on split-adjusted, dividend-unadjusted closes fetched for this purpose (a `~` marks a name computed from the dividend-adjusted cache only).
